## About

Hello, my name is Peter Pham. I am a recent graduate from the University of Washington studying Geography: Data Science. 
My work focuses on using spatial data and mapping technologies to analyze real-world problems, communicate geographic patterns, and support data-driven decision making.

Through my academic coursework and independent projects, I have developed experience working with geospatial data, spatial analysis, and interactive mapping tools. I regularly work with GIS platforms such as ArcGIS and QGIS to create maps, manage geographic datasets, and perform spatial analysis. I also have experience building web-based mapping applications using tools like Mapbox, which allow geographic data to be visualized interactively on the web.

My interests include spatial data visualization, environmental and urban analysis, and the integration of geospatial technologies with programming and web development. I enjoy combining technical tools with geographic insight to better understand spatial relationships and communicate complex information through maps and visualizations.

This portfolio highlights a selection of my GIS projects, including spatial analysis, web mapping applications, and research work. These projects demonstrate my experience working with geographic data, applying GIS techniques, and developing practical geospatial solutions.
